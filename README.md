# PRETERNATURAL

*A First-Person Psychological Horror Game*

**PRETERNATURAL** is a first-person psychological horror game set inside a mysterious research facility where reality begins to fall apart. Explore unsettling corridors, solve puzzles, collect useful tools, and survive encounters with an unknown creature as you search for a way out.

**Your goal is simple: escape. The way out is not.**

## About the Game

This place is not what it seems. Hallways stretch endlessly, unfamiliar doors lead to unknown places, and danger could be waiting around any corner. Explore carefully, pay attention to your surroundings, and think before you act. One wrong move might be your last.

## Story

You are an employee working at a research laboratory. One day, you discover a door that has never been there before.

Curiosity gets the better of you, and you decide to open it. The moment you do, you collapse and lose consciousness.

When you wake up, you find yourself in a corridor that never ends. The world you knew is gone, and you have no idea how you got there.

You have only one thing with you: a flashlight.

Something watches from the darkness. With no clear way back, you must explore the endless corridors, uncover their secrets, and find a way forward.

*Will you escape the forgotten facility, or will you be lost forever in the preternatural reality?*

## Gameplay

- **Explore:** Navigate strange environments and endless corridors.
- **Solve puzzles:** Find tools, interact with objects, and unlock blocked paths.
- **Survive:** Avoid a dangerous creature lurking in the facility.
- **Hide:** Use cabinets to escape the creature when you are being chased.
- **Discover:** Search for clues and uncover the mystery behind your surroundings.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `W` / `A` / `S` / `D` | Move |
| Mouse | Look around |
| `Left Shift` | Sprint |
| `F` | Toggle flashlight |
| `E` | Interact with objects |
| `Esc` | Open settings / pause menu |

## Features

- First-person psychological horror
- Atmospheric exploration and environmental storytelling
- Puzzle-solving and interactive objects
- A mysterious research facility with seemingly endless corridors
- Creature encounters and hiding mechanics
- A story centered around an unfamiliar and unsettling reality
